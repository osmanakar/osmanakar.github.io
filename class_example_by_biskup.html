General information: This is the 1st quarter of the 3-quarter graduate probability sequence. A rough plan what will be covered in this quarter is as follows:

  • Basic setup of probability theory: measure and integration, random variables and their distribution
  • Infinite product spaces, independent random variables, Borel-Cantelli lemmas, etc.
  • Laws of Large Numbers (weak and strong) for independent random variables
  • Convergence in distribution, Central Limit Theorem (characteristic function, Lindeberg's and Stein's methods)
  • Stable laws, infinite divisibility, limit laws for heavy-tailed random variables, Levy-Khinchin formula, laws of types
  • Conditional expectation and probability (time permitting)
A prerequisite for this course is a good knowledge of undergraduate analysis (up to Riemann integral, see my MATH131A notes and MATH131B notes); prior knowledge of measure theory will not be assumed. Still, if you want to get up to speed before we discuss measure in class, read e.g., Terry Tao's Introduction to Measure Theory or consult my MATH 245A notes.
